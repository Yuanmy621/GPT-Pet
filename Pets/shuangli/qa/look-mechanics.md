# 霜璃的视线动作

The dress hem, feet, lower body, wings, and curled tail stay anchored in the same front-facing stance. The head turns gently at the neck; the bangs, horns and attached hair rosette follow its rotation. The original lavender anime eyes keep their shape and lighting, with coordinated iris gaze and eyelid perspective. Keep the face visible in every pose and do not rotate the whole character.

Up: chin raised, visible face aimed upward, irises under the upper lids. Right: gentle head yaw toward screen-right, nose and irises right of head center; torso remains front-facing. Down: chin tucked, irises directed below center and lowered gaze still visible. Left: corresponding screen-left yaw and iris/nose displacement. Diagonals combine both physical axes with even angular steps. Hair movement remains small so body registration and silhouette do not pop.

The white dress and lavender ruffles are unchanged across directions. The rosette always remains on the same anatomical side. No new props, punctuation, arrows, light effects, scenery, or floating elements.
